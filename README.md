## 🌟 About HunarSetu

**HunarSetu** is a modern full-stack web platform designed to help students, learners, job seekers and aspiring professionals discover opportunities, develop relevant skills and receive structured career guidance.

The core idea behind HunarSetu is simple:

> **Every person has talent. The right guidance can turn that talent into opportunity.**

Many people have skills and potential but struggle to find:

- 🎯 The right career direction
- 📚 Relevant learning resources
- 💼 Suitable opportunities
- 🧠 Skill-development pathways
- 🧑‍💼 Professional guidance
- 🤝 Counselling support

HunarSetu brings these components together into one centralized digital ecosystem.

✨ Key Features
👤 User Platform

HunarSetu provides a simple and user-friendly interface where users can explore skills, learning resources, opportunities and career guidance.

🔎 Opportunity Discovery

Users can explore relevant opportunities based on their interests, skills and career goals.

📚 Skill Development

The platform helps users understand which skills can help them move toward their desired career path.

🎯 Career Guidance

Users can identify possible career directions and understand the steps required to reach their goals.

🧑‍💼 Counselling Support

Users can submit counselling-related requests and connect their needs with the counselling workflow.

🤖 AI-Powered Assistance

HunarSetu is designed with AI integration using the Google Gemini API ecosystem for intelligent and personalized assistance.
